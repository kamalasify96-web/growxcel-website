The Saudi opportunity

Why Saudi businesses need cost management now.

Three structural shifts are making cost visibility more urgent than ever for Saudi businesses across all sectors.

01

VAT & Regulatory Compliance

Saudi businesses now operate under a more complex tax and regulatory environment. Accurate cost systems are essential for compliance reporting, VAT allocation, and audit readiness — going beyond basic accounting.

02

ERP Investments Need ROI

Many Saudi companies have invested significantly in SAP, Oracle, or Odoo. Without a cost management layer, these systems generate data without generating decisions. We bridge the gap between ERP data and profit intelligence.

03

Competition Is Intensifying

As Vision 2030 opens Saudi markets and attracts international competition, margin management becomes a competitive necessity. Businesses that know their true costs can price strategically — others race to the bottom.

04

Talent & Saudization Pressures

As labour costs rise with Saudization requirements, operational efficiency becomes critical. We design cost systems that show exactly which activities, products, and branches are absorbing the most people cost relative to output.

05

F&B & Hospitality Boom

Saudi Arabia's F&B sector is growing rapidly — driven by tourism, entertainment, and demographic trends. Recipe costing, branch-level P&Ls, and procurement systems are essential tools for every F&B operator in KSA.

06

Healthcare & Medical Expansion

Saudi healthcare is expanding with new facilities and providers. Service-level cost management — from procedure costing to department P&Ls — is critical for new healthcare businesses operating in a complex pricing environment.
