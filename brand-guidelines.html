Voice & Tone

Growxcel speaks like the smartest person in the room who still gets to the point in one sentence.

Specific over vague

Never say "we improve your profitability." Say "we show you which branch is eating your margin." Specificity signals expertise.

Results-first language

Start with the outcome, not the service. "Margin visibility in 30 days" beats "we provide cost management consulting."

No fluff

Cut adjectives that don't add information. "Comprehensive", "holistic", and "best-in-class" are never needed. Let numbers speak.

Owner-to-owner

Speak directly to business owners and CFOs, not academic audiences. Use "your margin", "your team", "your branches."

System thinking

Frame everything as systems, not one-off projects. "We build systems that run after we leave" — not "we deliver reports."

Authority through credentials

Reference Dr. Rabab's PhD/DBA/CMA credentials and the team's specialization when establishing authority — never just "our experts."

Write Like This / Not Like That
✓ Write like this
Your accountant records history. We build systems that shape your future.
Short. Contrast-led. The juxtaposition does the work.
We show you which product is profitable and which one is quietly killing your margin.
Specific pain point. Direct accusation of the problem.
30+ years. 60+ clients. One formula.
Data anchors trust. "One formula" makes complexity simple.
Implementation happens inside your actual environment — not remotely.
The negative ("not remotely") makes the USP land harder.
SAP is running. Nobody's using it properly. We fix that.
Three punchy sentences. Diagnose, confirm, resolve.
✕ Never write like this
We provide comprehensive, holistic cost management solutions for businesses.
Vague, adjective-heavy, says nothing specific.
Our team of highly qualified experts will help you achieve your financial goals.
"Highly qualified" and "financial goals" are meaningless filler.
We are passionate about helping businesses grow and excel! 🚀
No emojis in professional copy. "Passionate" is a red flag word.
Feel free to reach out to learn more about our services and how we can help!
Passive and non-committal. Tell them what to do, not what they "can" do.
Growxcel is a leading consulting firm operating in the MENA region.
"Leading" is self-declared and unverifiable. Be specific instead.

Content Formats & Calendar

Six content formats. A weekly rhythm. Every post should teach, prove, or earn.

The Six Post Formats
01
The Profit Insight

One cost or margin concept explained in plain language. No jargon. Teach the audience something they can use today.

"Most restaurant owners calculate food cost wrong. They use purchase price — not actual consumption per dish. That 4% gap is where margin goes. Here's the right formula:"
02
The Before / After

Show the gap between what a client had and what Growxcel built. Always real results — never hypothetical.

"Client: F&B group, Saudi Arabia. Before: Revenue growing, margin shrinking. After: Cost architecture built for 12 branches. Margin visibility up 22% in 90 days."
03
The Founder Frame

Dr. Rabab shares a perspective from 30+ years of experience. First person, specific story or lesson — not generic advice.

"In 2018 I walked into a factory where the MD had no idea which product line was profitable. The ERP had been running for 3 years. The problem wasn't data — it was accountability structure."
04
The Question Post

A single diagnostic question that exposes a gap. No answer given — forces the reader to self-assess and comment.

"If your top salesperson left today — would your margin improve or worsen? Most owners can't answer this. That's the problem."
05
The Data Visual

A clean branded graphic with one data point or chart. Uses the dark brand palette. Always has a source or context label.

Dark background. White headline: "78% of SME costs are unmapped." Accent-blue bar chart. IBM Plex Sans body annotation. Growxcel wordmark bottom-right.
06
The On-Site Moment

Real photography from an engagement — team in the client's environment. No posed stock shots. Authentic operational settings.

"Day 3 on-site with a Cairo manufacturing client. Mapping 11 cost centres before we build the pricing model. This is where the work actually happens."

The Growxcel Caption Formula — Every Post

STEP 01
The Hook

First line = the most important. Must stop the scroll. Use a question, a surprising stat, or a direct challenge. Max 12 words.

STEP 02
The Problem

Name the specific pain. Make the reader feel seen — "this is happening in your business right now."

STEP 03
The Insight

Teach or prove something. One clear idea. Either a formula, a framework, or a real result. Specific numbers always win.

STEP 04
The CTA

One action only. "Comment below", "DM us 'AUDIT'", or "Book a cost audit at growxcel.com". Never two CTAs in one post.

Brand Don'ts

The fastest way to undermine a premium brand is small violations made consistently. These are absolute rules.

✕

Never use emojis as icons in any digital asset — website, social, email, or proposals

✕

Never use stock photography of generic business people, handshakes, or cityscapes

✕

Never describe Growxcel as "passionate", "holistic", "end-to-end", or "leading"

✕

Never use the danger red (#EF4444) in branding contexts — only in data visuals

✕

Never place white text on the accent blue (#4A7FD4) — contrast ratio is insufficient

✕

Never post without a clear CTA — every post must tell the reader one thing to do next

✕

Never use Bricolage Grotesque for body text, captions, or data labels — display use only

✕

Never claim specific client names in public posts without written client approval

✕

Never comment on competitor firms — ignore, don't engage negatively

✕

Never mix font roles — Space Grotesk headlines, IBM Plex Sans body. No exceptions.

✕

Never post personal or lifestyle content on the company LinkedIn page

✕

Never send unsolicited cold DMs promoting services on any social platform
