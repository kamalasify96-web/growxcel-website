Website policy

Privacy, in plain language.

This notice explains what happens to information you choose to share with Growxcel through this website.

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Information you provide

The website asks for business and contact details when you request a consultation. This may include your name, email address, phone number, business type, business size, and the operational issue you want to discuss.

How it is used

Growxcel uses this information only to understand your request, respond to you, prepare for a consultation, and maintain necessary business records. Growxcel does not sell the information you submit.

How the request is sent

The current form prepares an email in your own email application. Your information is not sent to Growxcel until you send that email. WhatsApp links are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

Retention and sharing

Information may be retained for as long as needed to respond, provide services, meet legal obligations, and maintain appropriate records. It may be shared with Growxcel team members and essential service providers only when needed for those purposes.

Your choices

You can ask Growxcel to correct or delete information you have submitted, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, email info@growxcel.com.
